Wavelength pricing on TTI-relevant routes (TG2026)

  • Marseille–Singapore 100G: only -2% CAGR 2021-24 — supply-constrained, Red Sea risk. Pricing leverage for SMW6 lit capacity.
  • Singapore–Tokyo 100G: -6% CAGR (Apricot/Bifrost/SJC-2 will accelerate).
  • Johannesburg–London 100G: $32,272/mo, -15% CAGR (Equiano impact). Still 5.9× London-NY.
  • Miami–São Paulo 100G: $12,000/mo, -25% CAGR (Firmina).
  • 100GigE IP transit floor: $0.05/Mbps/mo in lowest markets · 400GigE $0.08-0.09/Mbps (3.3× 100GigE).

Geopolitical chokepoints (TG2026)

  • Red Sea (Yemen): 2Africa, IEX, Africa-1, Raman, SMW6 awaiting deployment. Feb 2024 AAE-1/EIG/SEACOM faults caused 5-mo repair delay due to Houthi threat. Spurring Saudi terrestrial bypass (TEAS, EXA-SOCAR, WorldLink).
  • South China Sea: Chinese permits blocking new cables. ADC RFS late 2024, SJC-2 July 2025 after multi-year delays. Apricot routes east of Philippines to bypass.
  • US-China cable ban: No new direct PRC-US subsea. Boosts Echo, Bifrost via SE Asia.

Colocation rates (TG2026 H2 2025 median per kW)

  • Singapore: $600+/kW (moratorium era · 2.2% CAGR 4-yr capacity growth)
  • London / Frankfurt / Amsterdam: $450+/kW
  • São Paulo / Santiago / Querétaro / Bogotá: $400-460/kW
  • US cross-connects: ~3× European rates (in US, 5 XCs = 70% TCO hike)
  • Top DC markets by capacity: Northern Virginia 25M sqft · London 13.5M sqft · 26 markets >1M sqft + ≥10% 4-yr CAGR (7 in Asia incl. 4 in India)

Implications for TTI

  • Red Sea bypass is TTI's strategic moment: WorldLink, JADI, EXA-SOCAR routes through Türkiye benefit from chokepoint risk. SMW6 lit capacity will face less erosion.
  • Content provider share is dominant: ~75% of demand — TTI's wholesale model must shift toward hyperscaler IRU/dark-fiber partnerships (Meta, Google, MSFT, AWS).
  • Power-coupled DC era: 4+ yr wait → nuclear/SMR PPAs (AWS Cumulus, MSFT Crane, Google×Kairos). Türkiye DC market needs energy-security narrative.
  • Trans-Pacific surge ($3B+): Pacific Connect / Waterworth / Apricot / Bifrost are not direct competitors, but compress capacity prices on Europe-Asia routes via shifting hyperscaler workloads.
  • 400GigE adoption ramping (2-3 yrs): TTI ports must roll forward to stay relevant in EU hubs.
